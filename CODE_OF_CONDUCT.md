# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a friendly, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

**Expected behavior:** be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, welcome
newcomers, accept feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the project.

**Unacceptable behavior:** harassment, insults or personal attacks, discriminatory
language, publishing others' private information, and any conduct that would be
inappropriate in a professional setting.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues or contributors that violate this code.
Report concerns to [ggcaponetto@gmail.com](mailto:ggcaponetto@gmail.com); reports are
handled confidentially.

This document is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1.
